Features

Everything You Need to Hire

A complete AI-powered toolkit for modern recruiters built on the Google Workspace ecosystem.

🤖
Graded Job Descriptions
Drafts with the sections recruiters expect, then scores the result 0–100 and flags masculine-coded or exclusionary wording that narrows your applicant pool.
📊
Google Sheets Tracking
Every applicant, score, and decision is automatically logged to a dedicated Google Sheet.
📝
Auto Google Forms
Instantly creates an application form with resume upload — ready to share with candidates.
🔍
Verified Skill Matching
Embeddings shortlist plausible matches, then a model verifies each one — so "Java" is never mistaken for "Python" and "PyTorch" still counts as a deep-learning framework.
📧
Gmail Automation
Sends calendar invites, offer letters, and rejection emails automatically via Gmail API.
💼
LinkedIn Integration
Optionally post your job opening directly to LinkedIn with the application form link.
⚖️
Blind Screening
Name, contact details and gendered terms are stripped before any model sees the resume. Skills, dates and achievements stay intact.
📈
Measured, Not Asserted
The scorer is benchmarked against recruiter labels with Spearman, NDCG and precision@k — and against a keyword baseline it has to beat.
